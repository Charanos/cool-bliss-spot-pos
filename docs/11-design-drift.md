# Design drift register, Bliss

Where the build departs from the design documents, and why. Code cites these by number. A drift is
either a correction the documents should adopt, or a deliberate exception; each says which.

| No | Where | Drift | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Seat colours | Colour index is `(seat_no - 1) mod 8`, not `seat_no % 8` as docs/04 writes it | The palette table and every design file give Seat 1 Glacier; `% 8` would make it Ember and Seat 8 Glacier | Correction: docs/04 to adopt |
| D-02 | Floor product tile | A photograph band on the tile | The tile imagery decision for the Floor revamp; the category edge stays | Accepted exception |
| D-07 | Light focus colour | `focus` is glacier-600, not glacier-400 | glacier-400 measures 2.6:1 on frost-0, below the 3:1 floor for a focus indicator | Correction: docs/06 section 8 to adopt |
| D-08 | Light Shared outline | frost-600, not frost-300 | frost-300 measures 1.8:1 on frost-0 | Correction |
| D-11 | Floor order fire | Unfired markers lift and fade, staggered 16ms inside 120ms; rows stay | Fired lines remain on the rail as waiting, then poured | Correction: docs/07 to adopt |
| D-12 | Motion ceilings | `seat.total` (180ms) and the fire count-down (160ms) are clamped to 140ms | The Floor ceiling is the harder rule | Correction |
| D-13 | Lenis | Only `lerp` is set; `syncTouch: false` replaces `smoothTouch` | Lenis 1.3 has no `smoothTouch`, and `lerp` overrides `duration` | Correction |
| D-14 | Console surfaces | The Card family: an elevated card with bands for dashboards, grids and records, beside the flat Pane family for tables and forms | The Console's dashboards read better as cards; one level of depth still holds (docs/19 section 1) | Adopted in docs/06 section 1 |
| D-15 | Console type | A 14px Console ramp (`title-page`, `title-section`, `title-card`, `ui`, `label-caps`, `num-kpi`, `num-md`) | A dense desktop tool; the Floor's 15px floor stays for the Floor | Adopted in docs/06 section 3 |
| D-16 | Radius | `control` 12, `card` 16, `overlay` 20, `pill` for counts and segmented controls | Buttons were 10 to 16 by hand; dialogs 28 by hand | Adopted in docs/06 section 5 |
| D-17 | Status chip | On a wash of its tone, radius sm | The chip needs to read on a card surface; the dot and word still carry it in greyscale | Adopted |
| D-18 | Printed bill | No fiscal wording; "This bill is not a tax invoice" | KRA eTIMS is out of scope (docs/00); an invented fiscal footer was a false claim | Correction |
| D-19 | Console sign-in artwork | `artwork/console-flow.tsx` keeps literal gradient colours; `bliss/no-raw-hex` is off for that one file | It is a photographic illustration (a bottle's glass and foil, a pipe's brass): pigment stops, not interface colour. The other artworks draw from tokens | Accepted exception |
| D-20 | Floor bundle budget | The 220KB gzipped budget (ADR 003) is held against the station's entry bundle: the scripts a Floor or Counter page loads beyond the framework baseline every route shares, read from the not-found page. `scripts/bundle-budget.mjs` prints both figures. At this build the entry is 105 to 152KB and the whole first load 236 to 283KB, of which 131KB is React and the Next runtime | The budget exists to keep Console code and heavy libraries off the tablets; the framework is a fixed cost no Floor change can move. Lenis, ScrollTrigger and any Console chunk still fail the build outright | Correction: ADR 003 to adopt, and to revisit if the first load passes 300KB |
| D-21 | Console shell | The navigation sits on a sunken desk and the page on one floating sheet that scrolls on its own; a workspace's pages list under it on the desk and as pills at the head of each page | A flat rail and a top bar read as a template; the desk and sheet make one clear surface for the work, and keep the navigation still while the page moves | Adopted in docs/19 section 4 |
| D-22 | Console confirmations | The Console confirms a finished action with a toast at the foot of the sheet (a title in the button's own words, an optional line and way on). Refusals stay inline in the dialog that caused them; the Floor and Counter keep inline notices only | A manager changes many records in a row; a toast says it took without moving the page. The Floor's rule (feedback.tsx) stands for the tablets | Adopted in docs/19 section 3 |
| D-23 | Console cards | A card that needs a second look shows it with a tinted edge and a faint wash down from its head, never a bar down one side; chips inside the Console carry their word without the dot | The side bar read as a list marker and crowded cards in a grid; the word already names the state | Adopted in docs/19 section 3 |
| D-24 | Build targets | The build targets Safari 15.4, iOS 15.4 and Chrome, Edge and Firefox 111 (browserslist in package.json). Next's framework chunks are brought down to that after the build (`scripts/legacy/downlevel.mjs`); Tailwind's `color-mix` fallbacks become `rgb(var(--x-rgb) / n%)` (`packages/config/postcss-rgb-alpha.cjs`); `pnpm legacy` fails the build on anything Safari 15 cannot read | The Floor's reference device is an iPad mini 4, which stops at iPadOS 15.8 and so Safari 15.6. Next 16 and Tailwind v4 target Safari 16.4 by default, which left the router unparsed and every soft tint solid on that iPad | Adopted in docs/16 section 1 |
| D-25 | Display profiles | Each station sets `data-display` on its page: `standard`, `lite` (no blur, artwork or textures, nothing loops) and `clarity` (wider steps between dark surfaces, stronger status colours, 12px minimum type, edges in place of glows). They combine. The device check suggests one; the device keeps its choice | The iPad mini 4 cannot afford blur and artwork, and the counter laptop's panel washes colour out. A per-device profile fixes both without changing the look on a good screen. `contrast.ts` simulates a weak panel so Clarity is tested, not guessed | Adopted in docs/06 section 4 |
| D-26 | Profiles keep the design | Amends D-25. Every station starts in Clarity. Lite and Clarity no longer remove the sign-in photographs, the artwork, the textures or the blur behind a sheet: Lite draws the artwork once and makes only the small frosted surfaces solid; Clarity is colour, type and edges. The photographs are served from the app as WebP | On the iPad mini 4 and the counter laptop, stripping them made the stations look unfinished beside the Console; a still frame and one blur layer cost the A8 little, and Clarity reads well on every screen we have | Adopted in docs/06 section 4 |
| D-27 | One search, one dropdown | The Floor and the Counter share one search in the top bar (app/_pos/station-search.tsx): open tabs, named seats and the menu with prices and stock, dropping from the field; on a tab or a quick sale an item is added from it. Every dropdown on every surface is the shared listbox (listbox.tsx), never the operating system's menu | The Floor's dialog and the Counter's jump to Tabs did different things, and the system menu read as a plain box on a dark tablet | Adopted in docs/12 and docs/19 section 3 |
| D-28 | Shifts are recorded | A station sign-in starts the person's shift for the business day or joins the one running; signing out ends it and keeps its figures; tables handed over count on the giver's shift. A running shift's tabs, sales, voids and discounts are worked out as it is read. One left running is ended by a manager in the Console at the real time (modules/trade/shifts.ts) | Nothing but the seeded history wrote a shift, so the Shifts page was empty on a real night | Adopted; docs/14 section 1 |
